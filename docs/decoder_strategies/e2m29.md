# E2M29 strategy decision

E2M29 reserves exponent three for Inf/NaN and carries 29 fraction bits. Exact
decoding requires both FP64 register words. U(0,1) and roughly 68.27% of
N(0,1) use exponent zero, so CLZ normalization is a hot path.

Implemented candidates:

- generic codec control;
- branchy direct two-word construction with CLZ normalization;
- masked/predicated two-word construction;
- eight-entry sign/exponent prefix helper through global or shared memory,
  while exponent-zero values still use direct normalization;
- x1/x2/x4 packet loads and exploratory x8 composed from two x4 loads.

FP32 intermediates lose six fraction bits. Complete and subnormal LUTs would
require 32 GiB and 4 GiB, respectively, and are excluded. Validation covers
boundary classes and 200,000 deterministic pseudo-random encodings.
