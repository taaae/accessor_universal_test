# E3M28 strategy decision

E3M28 reserves exponent seven for Inf/NaN and carries 28 fraction bits. Exact
decoding needs both FP64 words. Its first normal magnitude is 0.25, so CLZ
normalization is exercised by 25% of U(0,1) and about 19.74% of N(0,1).

Implemented candidates:

- generic codec control;
- branchy direct two-word construction;
- masked/predicated two-word construction;
- 16-entry sign/exponent prefix helper through global or shared memory, with
  direct normalization for exponent zero;
- x1/x2/x4 packet loads and exploratory x8 composed from two x4 loads.

FP32 intermediates lose five fraction bits. Complete and subnormal LUTs would
require 32 GiB and 2 GiB and are excluded. The prefix helper is more plausible
than for E2M29 because regular exponents dominate both datasets. Validation
covers boundary classes and 200,000 deterministic pseudo-random encodings.
