# E11M20 strategy decision

E11M20 stores exactly FP64's high 32-bit word. Exact decoding is simply:

```cpp
__hiloint2double(int(raw), 0)
```

Implemented candidates:

- generic prefix-codec control;
- direct high-word insertion at x1/x2/x4;
- exploratory x8 implemented as two aligned x4 packets.

There is no field arithmetic and no special path: zero, source/FP64
subnormals, Inf, and NaN already occupy their final FP64 positions. FP32
intermediates are not exact over the exponent range, and even a high-word LUT
would require 16 GiB. Validation covers 200,000 deterministic encodings plus
boundary classes bit-for-bit.
