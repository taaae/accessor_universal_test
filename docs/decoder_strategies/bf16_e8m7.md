# BF16 E8M7 strategy decision

BF16 has the same sign and exponent fields as FP32. Its complete raw encoding
can therefore be shifted left 16 bits and reinterpreted as an exact FP32 value;
no field rebiasing or normalization is needed. Source subnormals are negligible
for U(0,1) and N(0,1).

Implemented candidates:

- generic native-codec control;
- scalar BF16-to-FP64 C++ conversion;
- scalar BF16-to-FP32-to-FP64 conversion;
- native BF16x2-to-`float2`, composed for x4/x8;
- raw BF16-bit lift to FP32 followed by FP64 conversion;
- branchy and masked direct FP64 high-word construction;
- complete 65,536-entry high-word LUT through global/L2;
- 128-entry, 512 B subnormal LUT through global or shared memory;
- x1/x2/x4/x8 aligned packet loads.

The raw-bit and native packed paths are the main candidates. Direct FP64 words
remove FP32-to-FP64 conversion but require more integer work. Local validation
exhausts all BF16 bit patterns against the exact shifted-FP32 reference.
