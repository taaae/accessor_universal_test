# E1M30 strategy decision

Finite E1M30 is a signed fixed-point grid with magnitude `K * 2^-29`. Its
31-bit magnitude converts exactly to FP64, but not to FP32. Direct decoding must
construct both 32-bit words of the FP64 representation when lower fraction bits
are nonzero.

Implemented candidates:

- generic codec control;
- fixed-point integer-to-FP64 plus exact `2^-29` scale;
- branchy and masked direct two-word FP64 construction;
- four-entry sign/exponent prefix helper through global or shared memory, with
  exponent-zero values normalized directly;
- x1/x2/x4 packet loads and exploratory x8 composed from two x4 loads.

Complete and subnormal LUTs are excluded: their exact sizes are 32 GiB and
8 GiB. FP32 intermediates are excluded because they discard seven source
fraction bits. Local validation covers boundary encodings and 200,000
deterministic pseudo-random codes exactly.
